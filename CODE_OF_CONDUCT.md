# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, constructive feedback,
accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community.
Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal
attacks, publishing private information, and other conduct inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's GitHub
Security reporting channel. Maintainers may remove or reject contributions,
comments, or participants whose conduct violates this policy.
